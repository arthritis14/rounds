Questions

Is there a Strava for drinking?

Yes. Rounds works like Strava for a night out: start a session, log each beer or cocktail as it arrives, and post the night with a graph, your pace and the time. Friends can cheer and comment, the way they give kudos on Strava.

Is there a Strava for beer?

Rounds has a beer mode for exactly that. Every pint, can or pitcher you log becomes a step on your graph, so you can see how the night went and compare it with your friends' nights.

Is Rounds free?

Yes. Rounds is free and runs in your phone's browser, so there's nothing to download from an app store.

Can I track cocktails as well as beer?

Yes. Choose beer, cocktails or both when you start. Cocktails, shots and wine all count as rounds.

Can I share my night on Instagram?

Yes. Every post can be exported as a transparent sticker with your graph and stats, ready to drop onto an Instagram story.

Is Rounds made by Strava?

No. Rounds is an independent app and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Strava, Inc. People just describe it as the Strava for drinking because it borrows the same idea.
